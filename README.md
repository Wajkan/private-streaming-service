# Private Streaming Service

*"This concept is built to give the market back to the people who creates it, free from forced tracking, data collection, algorithms and surveillance"*

The solution is simple, every person owns their own dedicated library, then we make it possible for everyone to buy digital copies, with the simplicity of a single click it gets into your library. 

This would create a free market where independent studios, smaller artists, film studios, production companies, record labels, etc. can flourish.
