# Private Streaming Service

*"Find what you love — buy via a direct payment to artists, creators — instantly accessed anywhere - from your own streaming service*

There is no easy way for me to buy a digital copy with one click that then becomes available on my own streaming service. This is the package, the simplicity of owning and buying INDEPENDENT of who sells it. 

By making this possible, the goal is to eliminate the need for central authority and revive creativity. Spreading the wealth between the parts involved and not someone who happend to create a nice UI that you can access.  

No subscriptions, no middlemen, no platform dependency.
